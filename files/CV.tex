\section{\footnotesize {\large R}ESEARCH {\large P}ROFILE}
Computer vision and machine learning researcher developing \textbf{predictive visual intelligence for robust embodied agents}. My work connects uncertainty-aware and data-efficient learning, temporal and 3D/4D scene understanding, vision--language--action models, and latent world models to prediction, planning, and control under distribution shift. As a Staff Researcher at Qualcomm AI Research, I lead projects in end-to-end autonomous driving, world-action modeling, multimodal learning, and dynamic 3D perception. My research has appeared in NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICML, WACV, IEEE TIP, and IEEE TPAMI and received the \textbf{Best Paper Award at IEEE ICIP 2021}.
